Built-in Cybersecurity Defenses

Rigorous defensive mechanisms safeguarding accounts and user identity

Entropy Calculation

Computes both theoretical pool size entropy (\(L \log_2 N\)) and Shannon character distribution entropy. Penalizes repetitive symbols to calculate true effective entropy bits.

Pattern & Keyboard Walks

Identifies alphabetical runs (abc, xyz), numerical progressions (1234, 9876), QWERTY keyboard sequences, dates, and repeated character runs.

Dictionary & Leetspeak Scan

Cross-references passwords against top leaked passwords and dictionary stems. De-obfuscates leetspeak substitutions (e.g. P@$$w0rd → password) to expose evasion attempts.

Bcrypt Hashing & Salting

Employs adaptive bcrypt with 12 computational cost rounds and per-user cryptographic salts, ensuring resistance against high-speed GPU and rainbow-table cracking attacks.

Brute-Force & Lockout

Monitors authentication attempts per username and IP. Temporarily locks accounts upon reaching 5 consecutive failures, with remaining attempt countdowns and automatic unlock expiration.

SOC Security Operations

Interactive administrator console with real-time Chart.js telemetry, suspicious login attempt alerts, one-click manual account lock/unlock, and CSV compliance audit log exports.
